Tone Deaf
Tennis

Same two paddles, same ball, same net. One change: you play it by humming. High note, paddle goes up. Low note, paddle goes down. Belt it and your next return is a smash.

Your opponent is Perfect Pitch Pete. Audio never leaves your browser: no uploads, no sign-up, no tracking. Headphones stop the sound effects confusing the mic.

Step 1 of 2

Hum your lowest comfortable note

Hold it steady for a couple of seconds. This becomes the bottom of the court.

—

Calibrated

C3 to A4

  • Higher note moves your paddle up
  • Lower note moves it down
  • Go quiet and it slowly sinks
  • Belt it as the ball arrives for a smash

First to 7. Your returns play the note you were humming, so every rally is a little song.
